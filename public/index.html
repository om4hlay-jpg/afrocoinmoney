Questions people ask first

I have never used crypto. Is this for me?

Yes. You never hold keys, never send a transaction yourself and never see a seed phrase. You fund a balance with crypto and withdraw back to an address you own.

Where does the daily return come from?

Each terminal you buy opens an automated trading session on our servers. Returns accrue against that session daily and are released into your wallet when the lock period matures.

What does a withdrawal cost?

Ten percent of the requested amount is held as a processing fee. Requesting USD 50 debits USD 50 and sends USD 45.

Can I hold more than one terminal?

No. One active terminal per account. When its lock period ends you can open another, usually a larger one.

Which currencies can I use?

Around forty, including UGX, KES, NGN, ZAR, GHS, RWF, TZS and USD. Your choice only affects how figures are displayed, never how they are stored or calculated.

What do you need from me to pay out?

Just a receiving address in the coin you want. We will never ask for your wallet password or recovery phrase.
